Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Morales Compositions LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Composed iOS app (the “App”).

If you have questions, contact us at composedsupport@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to save and sync your work, process purchases, and understand stability and general app usage. The App has no sign-in and no Composed account.

Your creations

Projects and compositions you create in the App (scores, chords, and related project data).

You keep the rights to your creations. We collect this content only so you can save and sync it. We do not sell it, license it, publish it, or share it with other users or third parties for their own use.

Purchases

  • App Store subscription and lifetime-purchase status, handled by Apple through StoreKit

Support

  • If you email composedsupport@gmail.com, we receive whatever you send, including your email address

Analytics and crash reports

If you allow it in iOS Settings, Apple may share diagnostics and usage information with us through App Store Connect. That can include device type, iOS version, app version, aggregated usage such as sessions and active devices, and crash logs.

It does not include the contents of your projects, and it does not include a record of which features you use. We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

What we do not collect today

  • Your name or email address (the App has no sign-in)
  • Location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Our own record of which features you use
  • Information from a third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or social-login SDK (the App does not include those)

Apple may collect information under its own policies when you use iCloud or the App Store.

2. How we collect information

Information How it is collected
Projects and compositions When you create or edit them; saved on your device and, if your device is signed in to iCloud, synced automatically to the App’s private database in your iCloud account
Purchase status Automatically by Apple StoreKit when you buy, restore, or manage a subscription
Support messages Only if you email us
Analytics and crash reports Provided by Apple through App Store Connect when you allow sharing in iOS Settings. Not taken from the contents of your projects.

We do not scrape data in the background for advertising or profiling.

3. Why we use information

  • Projects: save your work on your device and sync it across your devices that use the same iCloud account
  • Purchases: unlock Composed features you paid for
  • Support: reply to feedback
  • Analytics and crash reports: understand reliability and general usage, without using your compositions for those purposes

We do not use your information for advertising or selling data.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your projects or compositions with other users, advertisers, or third-party content platforms.

We use Apple services that process data in order to provide the App:

  • iCloud / CloudKit — private sync of your projects through the iCloud account on your device (other Composed users cannot see them)
  • App Store / StoreKit — purchases and subscriptions
  • App Store Connect — aggregated analytics and crash reports, when you allow sharing in iOS Settings

If you choose to, you may also open:

  • Apple Mail — to contact support
  • Instagram — if you tap @cole.composed

Those services are optional and follow their own privacy policies.

We do not use a third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK.

5. How long we keep data and how to delete it

  • Projects: kept on your device, and in the App’s data in your iCloud account if iCloud is on, until you delete them. Copies may also remain in your own device backups (such as iCloud Backup) until those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • Purchases: Apple keeps App Store records for your Apple ID. Deleting your data in the App, or deleting the App, does not cancel a subscription or lifetime purchase.
  • Support emails: kept as long as needed to handle your request, then according to our normal email practices.
  • Analytics and crash reports: kept by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

To delete a single project: delete it in the project list. That removes it from this device and from the App’s data in your iCloud account. If iCloud is unavailable at that moment, the iCloud copy is deleted the next time the App runs with iCloud available. A small marker with no project content stays in your iCloud account so your other devices remove the project too. If another of your devices has changes to that project that had not synced yet, those changes are kept and the project comes back.

To delete all of your project data in the App: open Settings in the App and tap Delete All Data, then confirm twice.

That removes your projects from this device and from the App’s data in your iCloud account. If iCloud is unavailable at that moment, the iCloud copy is deleted the next time the App runs with iCloud available. Deletion cannot be undone.

Deleting the App removes the projects stored on that device but leaves the App’s data in your iCloud account, so your projects come back if you reinstall the App. To remove that data without the App, open iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage (called Manage Account Storage on some iOS versions). If Composed is listed, tap it and choose Delete Data from iCloud. Apple’s menu names can vary between iOS versions.

To cancel a subscription: iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or Manage in the App’s Settings. Deleting your data does not cancel it.

For questions or requests about your data, email composedsupport@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and consent

The App does not use tracking or advertising, so there is no separate advertising-consent or tracking prompt.

You can:

  • Delete individual projects in the project list, or all project data with Settings → Delete All Data (see section 5)
  • Turn off iCloud for Composed in iOS Settings (your projects are then saved on this device only)
  • Turn Apple’s diagnostics sharing off or on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers
  • Choose whether to email us or open Instagram

Access, correction, export, and deletion

  • Access and correction: your projects are available in the App, where you can open, edit, and rename them at any time.
  • Export: you can export a project as WAV, MIDI, Score PDF, or MusicXML from its options menu in the project list.
  • Deletion: delete individual projects in the project list, or everything with Settings → Delete All Data, as described in section 5.

Your projects are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access, so we cannot view, copy, or delete them for you. For information we do hold, such as support emails, email composedsupport@gmail.com to ask for access, correction, or deletion.

No sign-in is needed to use the App. If your device is signed in to iCloud, your projects sync automatically; if not, they are saved on this device only.

7. Children’s privacy

Composed is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact composedsupport@gmail.com and we will delete any such information we hold. Project data on a device and in its iCloud account can be removed with Settings → Delete All Data.

8. International users

The App is provided from the United States. If you use it from another country, your information may be processed on Apple’s iCloud and App Store infrastructure in the United States or other regions Apple operates.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. If we later add our own analytics, or a provider other than Apple, we will update this policy before that collection starts.
