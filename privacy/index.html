Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Morales Compositions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Composed iOS app (the “App”).

If you have questions, contact us at composedsupport@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run your account, save your work, process purchases, and understand stability and feature use.

Account

  • A Sign in with Apple user identifier
  • Your name, if Apple provides it on first sign-in (we do not request your email from Sign in with Apple)

Your creations

Projects and compositions you create in the App (scores, chords, and related project data).

You keep the rights to your creations. We collect this content only so you can save and sync it. We do not sell it, license it, publish it, or share it with other users or third parties for their own use.

Purchases

  • App Store subscription and lifetime-purchase status, handled by Apple through StoreKit

Support

  • If you email composedsupport@gmail.com, we receive whatever you send, including your email address

Analytics and crash reports

We collect analytics events and crash reports to understand stability and how features are used.

This can include device type, iOS version, app version, usage events, and crash logs. It does not include the contents of your projects. We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

What we do not collect today

  • Email address via Sign in with Apple
  • Location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Information from advertising or social-login SDKs (the App does not include those)

Apple may collect information under its own policies when you use Sign in with Apple, iCloud, or the App Store.

2. How we collect information

Information How it is collected
Sign in with Apple ID and name When you tap Sign in with Apple
Projects and compositions When you create or edit them; saved on your device and, if iCloud is available, synced to your iCloud private database
Purchase status Automatically by Apple StoreKit when you buy, restore, or manage a subscription
Support messages Only if you email us
Analytics events and crash reports Automatically while you use the App, through our analytics and crash-reporting service. Not taken from the contents of your projects.

We do not scrape data in the background for advertising or profiling.

3. Why we use information

  • Account: keep you signed in and restore your session
  • Projects: save your work and sync it across your devices via iCloud
  • Purchases: unlock Composed features you paid for
  • Support: reply to feedback
  • Analytics and crash reports: improve reliability and the product, without using your compositions for those purposes

We do not use your information for advertising or selling data.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your projects or compositions with other users, advertisers, or third-party content platforms.

We use Apple services that process data in order to provide the App:

  • Sign in with Apple — authentication
  • iCloud / CloudKit — private sync of your projects (other Composed users cannot see them)
  • App Store / StoreKit — purchases and subscriptions

If you choose to, you may also open:

  • Apple Mail — to contact support
  • Instagram — if you tap @app.composed

Those services are optional and follow their own privacy policies.

We share analytics events and crash reports with our analytics and crash-reporting provider, which processes them for us for the purposes above. We do not use advertising SDKs. If we change that provider, we will update this policy.

5. How long we keep data and how to delete it

  • Account and projects: kept until you delete your account or delete the projects. Signing out leaves your iCloud projects in place so you can sign back in.
  • Purchases: Apple keeps App Store records for your Apple ID. Deleting your Composed account does not cancel a subscription or lifetime purchase.
  • Support emails: kept as long as needed to handle your request, then according to our normal email practices.
  • Analytics and crash reports: kept only as long as needed to understand stability and feature use.

To delete your account and project data in the App: Settings → Delete Account & Data

That removes your projects from this device and from your Composed iCloud data, then signs you out.

To disconnect Sign in with Apple: Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple → Composed → Stop Using Apple ID

To cancel a subscription: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (or Manage in the App)

You can also email composedsupport@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and consent

The App does not use tracking or advertising, so there is no separate advertising-consent or tracking prompt. Analytics and crash reporting described above are part of using the App.

You can:

  • Sign out in Settings
  • Delete your account and project data as described above
  • Stop Using Apple ID in iOS Settings
  • Turn off iCloud for Composed in iOS Settings
  • Choose whether to email us or open Instagram

Using Sign in with Apple is required to use the App.

7. Children’s privacy

Composed is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact composedsupport@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. International users

The App is provided from the United States. If you use it from another country, your information may be processed on Apple’s iCloud and App Store infrastructure, and by our analytics and crash-reporting provider, in the United States or other regions those services operate.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. If we share data with a new provider, we will update this policy to name that provider.
